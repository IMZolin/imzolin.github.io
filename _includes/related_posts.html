{%- comment -%} Related articles as an Obsidian-style graph (drawn by assets/js/related-graph.js). A post is related to the current one when - one post links to the other (a mention, e.g. via post_url), or - the two posts share at least one tag. The graph holds the current post plus its related posts; edges between any two of them are drawn too (solid = mention, dashed = shared tag). Clicking a node opens it. Mentions rank first, then shared tags.
